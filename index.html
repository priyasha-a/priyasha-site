About

I'm an MBA candidate at the Wharton School, where I'm majoring in Artificial Intelligence for Business. Before Wharton I spent three years at Bain & Company in San Francisco, working on go-to-market strategy, pricing, and technology due diligence — mostly for software and consumer companies.

Before that I studied Economics and Statistics & Data Science at UC Santa Barbara. I'm interested in what actually changes when AI lands inside a business: how products get sold, how teams are organised, and which of those changes turn out to matter.

Outside of that I dance, play a great deal of GeoGuessr, and have a weakness for cryptograms and killer sudokus. The flag quiz below started as a way to get better at the geography half of that.
